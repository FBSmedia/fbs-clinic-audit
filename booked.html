Before you join

The quick questions

Is this a sales call?

It's a look at your numbers. Our consultant walks you through where your clinic is losing booked consults and what each leak is worth. You keep the breakdown either way, and if there's nothing worth fixing, we'll tell you that too.

Do you touch patient data on the call?

No. We only look at how enquiries reach your clinic and get booked, the business side. Never patient records or anything clinical.

How long until we're live?

You go live within a week of your intake form and patient list coming in. We build and connect everything, and your team keeps working as normal.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your clinic. Our consultant explains the plans on the call, once you've seen your numbers, so you can decide with them in front of you.
